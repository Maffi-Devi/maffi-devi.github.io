The story

From arts student
to tech founder.

My career in technology didn't start in a computer lab. In 2020 I was a mathematics undergraduate with very little computer experience — and the lockdown made one thing obvious: the future was digital, and I was being left behind.

So I enrolled in NIELIT's O-Level course, earned an S-Grade in Python, became a certified Trainer of Trainers — and started teaching what I had just learned. Four years and 2,000+ students later, I noticed the same AI I was teaching could solve a problem I saw every week: ordinary people losing money to scams.

That became DigitalKawach. Building it turned me from an instructor into a builder, and A&N Web Services is how I now bring that engineering to businesses — while completing my MCA at Kurukshetra University.

"I don't just want to use AI — I want to build systems that act as a proactive guardian for every internet user."
