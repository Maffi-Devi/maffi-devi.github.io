About

Developer, founder and trainer

I am an AI web engineer from Kaithal, Haryana, India. I am the founder and lead developer of A&N Web Services, the developer of the scam-detection app DigitalKawach, and an AI and cyber security trainer.

I started in technology in 2020 as a mathematics undergraduate with very little computer experience. I completed NIELIT's O-Level course with an S grade in Python, qualified as a certified trainer, and began teaching at Zed-King Group of Institutions in July 2022.

DigitalKawach: AI-based scam detection

DigitalKawach checks SMS, calls, links, WhatsApp messages and email for signs of fraud and explains the risk in plain language. It is designed for everyday users such as students, parents and senior citizens. The project qualified for Round 2 of the India AI Impact Buildathon 2026.

Features
SMS dashboard, call analysis, URL scanner, phone-number lookup, WhatsApp and email scanners
Detection
Three layers: scam-pattern rule engine, LLM analysis (Groq) and VirusTotal URL reputation
Backend
Flask API with Gmail OAuth (read-only access), deployed on Render
Privacy
User messages are analysed but never stored
